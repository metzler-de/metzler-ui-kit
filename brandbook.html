Metzler · Brandbook · Introduction

Introduction

This brandbook translates the Metzler design system into clear rules for every touchpoint — website, print, packaging, and marketing. It is the single source of truth: colors, typography, logo usage, and composition all live here. Use it as a reference when creating campaigns, listing new products, or preparing materials for our print vendors.

Who it's for

Marketing managers, content editors, product photographers, print vendors, and anyone else representing Metzler externally. No design background required — every rule is written to be followed, not interpreted.

How to use it

Each chapter covers one system — color, logo, typography, layout. Rules are paired with a visual example and a do / don't comparison. When in doubt, follow the rule rather than your instinct. Consistency is what makes the brand recognisable.

What's inside

Primary color system · Logo variations · Typography hierarchy · Logo construction & proportions · Approved color variants · Incorrect usage reference. All values are sourced from the Metzler design system Figma library.

Typography

Our primary typeface is Helvetica Neue — the neutral, engineered sans-serif that carries the Metzler brand across print, web, product, and packaging. Its geometry is quiet so the engineering of the doorbell can speak for itself.

The family ships in six weights — Thin, Light, Regular, Medium, Bold, and Black — giving us the flexibility to build hierarchy from tiny caption labels all the way up to oversized editorial headlines. It reads well at every size, in every medium.

Typography Weights

Setting type with a mindful hierarchy keeps copy easy to read and navigate, helps our key messages stand out, and creates an attractively designed page. We create hierarchy through variations in type position, weight, size, capitalization, and color — never through decoration or effects.

Logo
Versions

The Metzler logotype exists in four approved versions. Red + Black is the default mark used across digital and print. Red + White is the reversed lockup for dark backgrounds and full-bleed brand color fills.

Monochrome white is used for engraving (Gravur) on dark metal or plastic, for embossing on colored stock, and for any print application where only a single light tone is available.

Monochrome black is used for single-color printing — newspapers, fax, stamps, laser engraving on light surfaces — and anywhere the press only renders one ink.

Note

Never place a colored M-square on a colored background and never recolor the wordmark outside the approved set. When in doubt, default to the monochrome black or white version.

Incorrect
Logo Usage

The Metzler logo is never redrawn, recolored, or decorated. Always reproduce it from the approved master file, at the correct proportions, in one of the four approved color variants.

Distorting the lockup — stretching, squashing, rotating, or rearranging the M-square and wordmark — breaks the geometry that gives the mark its balance.

Unapproved colors and effects — tints, gradients, drop shadows, outer glows, outlines — are never permitted, even for decorative layouts.

Metzler Gradient

The Metzler gradient is a calm transition between the deep teal of Primary Dark and the lighter Primary Hover. It represents the movement from foundation to clarity — from the depth of heritage to the forward momentum of modern advisory. The gradient is used primarily in backgrounds and large-format compositions to create depth and continuity. It may be applied to section openers, key visual areas, or transitions between content blocks, always in a restrained manner that preserves readability and a calm visual rhythm.

Biometric Access · Gesichtserkennung

Ablauf der
Gesichtserkennung.

Schneller Zutritt, sicher gelöst. Die Dual-Kamera erfasst ein dreidimensionales Gesichtsmodell in unter einer Sekunde und vergleicht die Merkmale mit lokal gespeicherten Profilen — zuverlässig bei jedem Lichtverhältnis.

1

Zwei Kameras erfassen das Gesicht dreidimensional und wandeln es in digitale Daten um.

2

Charakteristische Merkmale des Gesichts werden identifiziert und gespeichert.

3

Beim Scanvorgang werden die erfassten Merkmale mit existierenden Daten verglichen.

4

Ein Algorithmus überprüft, ob das gescannte Gesicht mit bereits gespeicherten Daten übereinstimmt.

Do / Don't Quick Reference

A single-page scan of the rules that matter most when anyone outside the brand team ships Metzler work. Print it, pin it by the designer's desk. When in doubt, default to the column on the left.

Always do

Use the logo only on white or approved brand backgrounds.
Maintain the defined clear space around the logo at all times.
Use approved HEX values — never eyedrop from a photo.
Scale images proportionally, never stretch or squish.
Use one typeface family per document — Helvetica Neue.
Left-align body text in multi-column layouts.
Use spacing multiples of the 4 px base unit for all padding.
Deliver print files as CMYK PDF/X-1a with 3 mm bleed.

Never do

Stretch, squish, rotate, or add effects to the logo.
Place the logo on busy or low-contrast backgrounds.
Use more than 2 accent colors on a single page.
Use ALL CAPS for body text or headings longer than 5 words.
Mix icon libraries — Lucide only, never Material or Font Awesome.
Center-align text blocks longer than 3 lines.
Submit RGB or PNG files to print vendors.
Add drop shadows, glows, or outlines to logos or icons.
