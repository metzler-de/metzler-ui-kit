### 1. Get every product to at least ~5 reviews; prioritise high-price items
- **Why:**
  - Purchase likelihood with five reviews is **270 %** higher than with none.
  - Reviews lift conversion **190 %** for lower-priced products and **380 %** for higher-priced ones.
  - Source: https://spiegel.medill.northwestern.edu/how-online-reviews-influence-sales/
- **Metzler, now:**
  - Bestsellers are well covered.
  - **XDM10 (899–1.649 €) has zero reviews.** That is the highest-price, highest-benefit case.
- **Metzler, recommended:**
  - Run a targeted post-install review request for intercom buyers, sent 3–4 weeks after delivery to allow for installation.
  - Show "Noch keine Bewertung – seien Sie der Erste" plus the shop rating, clearly labelled.

### 2. Don't hide negatives; a score near 5,0 reads as less credible
- **Why:**
  - Purchase likelihood typically peaks at **4,0–4,7** stars and falls as the rating approaches 5,0. https://spiegel.medill.northwestern.edu/how-online-reviews-influence-sales/
  - "53% of users … actively sought out the negative reviews". https://baymard.com/blog/user-ratings-distribution-summary
- **Metzler, now:** several products show 5,0 (rounded). The distribution is visible, which helps.
- **Metzler, recommended:**
  - Show one decimal place based on the real average (e.g. 4,9).
  - Keep the 1–3★ filter links visible.
  - Never pre-filter reviews by stars.

### 3. Label verified purchases, and explain how verification works (legal duty)
- **Why:**
  - A "verified buyer" badge improves purchase odds by **15 %**. Spiegel, as above.
  - **Law:** § 5b Abs. 3 UWG makes "whether and how" the shop checks that reviews come from real buyers a "wesentliche Information". https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/uwg_2004/__5b.html
  - UWG Anhang Nr. 23b and 23c ban false "verified" claims and fake reviews. https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/uwg_2004/anhang.html
- **Example:** each Otto review shows "Verifizierter Kauf", and the page states that only customers who ordered and received the item can publish. https://www.otto.de/kundenbewertungen/C858945366/
- **Metzler, now:** the label "Geprüfte Kundenbewertungen" conflicts with the many "Gast" authors and with the account-only rule stated on /kundenbewertungen.
- **Metzler, recommended:**
  - Next to every review block, add a one-line note linking to a method page. Wording to use: "Bewertungen stammen von Käufern, die wir per Bestell-Link einladen; jede Bewertung ist einer Bestellung zugeordnet."
  - Show "Verifizierter Kauf" per review only where that is technically true.
  - Fix the "Gast" versus account-only contradiction.

### 4. Ask for text and photos (installed product, engraving close-up)
- **Why (PowerReviews 2023, 8.153 US consumers; vendor research):**
  - 56 % don't trust a star rating without text.
  - 77 % trust customer imagery.
  - 45 % won't buy a product with no reviews.
  - Source: https://www.powerreviews.com/power-of-reviews-2023/
- **Why (Baymard):** 63 % of sites don't let users browse reviews by customer images. https://baymard.com/blog/current-state-ecommerce-product-page-ux
- **Metzler, now:** customer photos already appear in "Kundenstimmen". Strong.
- **Metzler, recommended:**
  - Add a "Nur Bewertungen mit Fotos" filter.
  - Feed review photos into the PDP gallery as a "Kundenfotos" tile. Get consent first.

### 5. Reply publicly to negative reviews
- **Why:** 89 % of sites don't. A participant on the German site Cyberport said replies show the shop cares. https://baymard.com/blog/current-state-ecommerce-product-page-ux
- **Example:** the Thomann Trusted Shops profile shows merchant replies and a "Verifiziert" label on each review. https://www.trustedshops.de/bewertung/info_X2ACE03BEC3651F5721FF55FE816CEC63.html
- **Metzler, now:** not verified whether replies are shown on PDP reviews.
- **Metzler, recommended:** show "Antwort von Metzler" under every 1–3★ review, signed with a first name.

### 6. Publish your moderation rules
- **Example:** Thomann's FAQ covers approval within 48 h, which checks are applied, and that reviewers can edit or delete their own review. https://www.thomann.de/de/faq_question_wie_geht_thomann_mit_bewertungen_um.html
- **Metzler, recommended:** expand /kundenbewertungen into a method page:
  - who can review
  - how invitations work
  - moderation rules
  - whether negative reviews are published (they must be)
  - how the aggregated score is calculated across Trusted Shops, Google and Trustpilot

### 7. Third-party seals must be live and link to the profile
- **Why:** NN/g credibility factor "connected to the rest of the web". https://www.nngroup.com/articles/trustworthy-design/ No conversion figure for seals was verified.
- **Metzler, now:** "Trusted Shops Käuferschutz" is a text claim with no trustbadge script.
- **Metzler, recommended:**
  - Either embed the official Trusted Shops trustbadge or seal (verifiable, links to the certificate), or rename the USP so it doesn't imply a seal.
  - Link the "4,7 ★" element to the Trusted Shops profile.

### 8. Structured data: product reviews only, and only if visible
- **Why:** marked-up reviews must be visible on the page. Self-serving LocalBusiness or Organization ratings are not eligible for review snippets. https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/review-snippet
- **Metzler, recommended:**
  - Emit AggregateRating only on PDPs that show product reviews.
  - Never put the 36.785 shop rating into Product markup.
  - Cautionary example: gravuru emits a kk-star-ratings vote count as AggregateRating while its review tab says "Es gibt noch keine Rezensionen" (see `../competitors.md`).

### 9. Make claims consistent and current everywhere
- **Why:** NN/g credibility factors include "comprehensive, current" content and up-front disclosure. https://www.nngroup.com/articles/trustworthy-design/
- **Metzler, now:**
  - Customer count: 2 Mio. (homepage), 1.800.000 (/ueber-uns), 1.000.000 (category SEO text).
  - ntv: "vierte Jahr in Folge" (homepage) vs "3 Jahre … 2025" (/auszeichnungen).
  - "Made in Germany" (Stella copy) vs "Designed in Germany" (XDM10, homepage).
  - The ntv "für Briefkästen" box is shown on the Türklingel category.
- **Metzler, recommended:**
  - Keep one claims register (number, source, date, wording) and reference it from new pages.
  - Say "Made in Germany" only where it is true for that product.

### 10. Be precise about guarantee and returns on the PDP
- **Why:** 44 % of sites don't link the return policy from the PDP. https://baymard.com/blog/current-state-ecommerce-product-page-ux
- **Example:** the Thomann buy box shows "30 Tage Money-Back-Garantie · 3 Jahre Thomann Garantie". https://www.thomann.de/de/harley_benton_hbd120bk_westerngitarre.htm
- **Metzler, now:**
  - "10 Jahre Metzler Garantie" appears generically, including on XDM10 electronics.
  - It actually covers Durchrostung only, for Metzler-brand products, for DE/AT consumers, on orders from 01.09.2022.
- **Metzler, recommended:**
  - Use "10 Jahre Garantie gegen Durchrostung" on steel products, with a link.
  - On electronics, show the actual warranty.
  - Add a returns line, noting that engraved items are excluded.

### 11. Put the most trust on unfamiliar, expensive products (intercoms)
- **Why:**
  - Spiegel: +380 % for higher-priced products (rule 1).
  - PowerReviews (vendor): 78 % read more reviews the more expensive the product.
- **Metzler, now:** the XDM10 buy box has a service block: "Designed in Germany", Garantie, FAQ, "Fachhandels- und Montagepartner – Partner in Ihrer Nähe finden", "Beratungstermin vereinbaren – Kostenlos & unverbindlich". Good.
- **Metzler, recommended:**
  - Add the Red Dot badge (linked).
  - Add a named contact with photo and the Sprechanlagen-Hotline.
  - Add "Installations-Service in Ihrer Nähe".
  - Add a case study: "Altbau, 2-Draht nachgerüstet in 2 Std."

### 12. D2C: show independent proof on the page
- **Why:** 62 % of DTC users say they would look for third-party reviews, and 29 % actually left the site to find them. https://baymard.com/blog/user-reviews-dtc
- **Metzler, recommended:**
  - Show "Auch auf Amazon: 4,5 ★ aus 1.636 Bewertungen", linked, and only if Amazon's brand guidelines allow it.
  - Show press and test seals with links.
  - This keeps users on the site instead of sending them off to search.
