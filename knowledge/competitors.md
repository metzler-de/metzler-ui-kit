## 1. Competitor profiles

### 1.1 gravuru — https://gravuru.de (closest D2C rival for engraved doorbells)

**Who:** gravuru GmbH, Bielefeld. WooCommerce shop. It also sells on Amazon under its own button brand "CHRISCK design".

**Sells:**
- Edelstahl, Schiefer and Acryl doorbells (LED options, Mehrfamilien).
- House numbers and signs.
- Memorial plates, key rings, gifts.
- Its Briefkasten category is **empty** ("Es wurden keine Produkte gefunden"): https://gravuru.de/kategorie/briefkasten/edelstahl-briefkasten-mit-gravur/

**Price vs Metzler:** at or below Metzler. Its 33 Edelstahl doorbells range from 23,99 to 62,99 €.

| Product | Price | URL |
|---|---|---|
| Klingel Edelstahl 13×26 cm, Wunschgravur + LED-Taster | ab 45,99 € | https://gravuru.de/produkt/edelstahl-klingel-wunschgravur-taster-13x26-cm/ |
| Türklingel mit beleuchtetem Klingelschild 12×9 cm | ab 58,99 € | https://gravuru.de/produkt/beleuchtete-tuerklingel-mit-gravur-und-led-klingelschild-12x9-cm/ |
| Hausnummer Edelstahl mit Gravur 30×20 cm | 89,99 € | https://gravuru.de/produkt/hausnummer-aus-edelstahl-mit-gravur-30x20-cm/ |
| CHRISCK design Edelstahl-Türklingel on Amazon | 24,99 €, 4,5★ / 51 | https://www.amazon.de/dp/B07H5S84XY |

**Standout UX:**
- **Free-form editor.** "Jetzt gestalten" opens a full-screen Fancy Product Designer canvas with: "Text hinzufügen", "Motive", "Schriftart", "in Kurve" (curved text), "Drehen", "Position" and letter spacing. "In den Warenkorb" sits inside the editor. Verified in the browser.
- **Add-on ladder on the PDP:**
  - LED colour (6 options)
  - "mit Stecker" +3,99 €
  - 8 Befestigung options, +2,89 to +8,99 €
  - Acrylic Dekorplatte +5,00 €
- **Delivery:**
  - Homepage claim: "Blitzschneller Versand – meist noch am nächsten Werktag".
  - PDP tab: "Inland (Deutschland): 1-3 Werktage".
  - Shipping: 6,90 €, free from 29,99 € (per the competitor report).
- **Payment:** Rechnung, Klarna, PayPal/Mollie, Vorkasse.
- **Other:** price filter on categories; Facebook/Google social login.

**Weaknesses:**
- The PDP rating "4.3/5 – 59 Bewertungen" (emitted as AggregateRating) comes from a kk-star-ratings vote widget. The review tab on the same page says "Es gibt noch keine Rezensionen". No Trusted Shops or Google seal was found.
- The delivery promise is generic, with no date.
- Gift-shop range dilutes the brand.
- No mailboxes or intercoms.

**Metzler should copy / beat:**
- Offer **motifs + curved text + free positioning** as an "Experten-Modus" in the configurator.
- Show the add-on ladder as visible PDP options, not hidden accessories.
- Out-trust them: put Metzler's real, verified review counts on every card and PDP. gravuru has none it can substantiate.

### 1.2 Frabox — https://www.frabox.de (strongest all-round D2C rival)

**Who:** frabox.de GmbH, Paderborn. A multi-brand retailer (including Knobloch) that also has its **own "Frabox Design" line**. Its categories mirror Metzler's: Briefkasten, Paketbox, Briefkastenanlagen, Türklingel & Video, Hausnummern, Außenleuchten.

**Price vs Metzler:** doorbells at or above Metzler's entry level; mailboxes and parcel boxes comparable.

**Standout UX:**
- **Live 2D engraving preview.** Under "Design & Beschriftung" → "Ihre Konfiguration" the customer sets:
  - "Schriftart" by real font name (Eurostile, Avantgarde Md BT, Arial, Monotype Corsiva)
  - "Name" and "Name (2. Zeile)"
  - "Gravurposition" (unterhalb / oberhalb des Tasters)
  
  Typed text appears on the product image straight away. Verified in the browser.
- **Colour:** 6 RAL swatches + "RAL Farbe nach Wahl".
- **Delivery on PDP and cards:**
  - PDP: "Lieferzeit ca. 2 - 3 Werktage"
  - Listing cards: "nur 2 - 3 Werktage" / "nur 5 Werktage"
- **Header USP strip:** "Kauf auf Rechnung", "Versandkostenfrei ab 30 EUR (in D)", "30 Tage Rückgaberecht", "Kostenlose Retoure".
- **Trusted Shops:** shop **4,76 / 16.654 Bewertungen** (https://www.trustedshops.de/bewertung/info_X6D429690D85CBA27F0C7D57DDA6546A0.html). Product reviews also run through Trusted Shops (STOKE LED: ⌀ 4,8 / 128). Review counts appear on every listing card.
- **PDP downloads:** Bohrschablone, Anschlussanleitung LED-Taster, Montageanleitung.
- **Guarantees:** "20 Jahre Frabox-Garantie gegen Durchrostung" + 5 years manufacturer warranty.

**Weaknesses:**
- The preview is a flat overlay with fixed layout ("Anordnung erfolgt wie im Musterbild").
- Only 4 fonts, no motifs, no 3D.

**Metzler should copy / beat:**
- Show a delivery estimate on **cards**, not only on the PDP.
- Put review counts on cards. Metzler's cards already show counts; keep them.
- Downloads block with a **Bohrschablone**.
- Name fonts by their real names.
- Metzler's 3D beats Frabox's 2D only where it exists (~15%). Ship at least a 2D live overlay on every engravable product.
- Rust guarantee: Frabox says 20 years, Metzler 10. Don't headline the guarantee length in comparisons.

## 3. Positioning summary

### Where Metzler leads
1. **Review volume and awards.**
   - 36.785 aggregated reviews, against Frabox's 16.654 on Trusted Shops and Knobloch's 529.
   - 739 reviews on Siebert alone.
   - 1.636 Amazon ratings.
   - ntv four years in a row, TopShop, Red Dot 2026.
   - No direct D2C rival comes close.
2. **3D live engraving preview** on the products that have it. Only Elcom (trade) and DoorBird do 3D; nobody in engraved mailboxes does.
3. **Full range from one maker:** Klingel, Briefkasten, Paketbox, Sprechanlage, Hausnummer, Mülltonnenbox, Leuchten. Only Knobloch and Frabox are comparable.
4. **Intercoms you can buy online with a guided Kaufberater.** Siedle, Ritto, Elcom, 2N and ABUS all force a dealer or quote. Metzler sits between DoorBird (similar price, slower delivery) and budget kits (Hikvision, Balter).
5. **Fast dispatch:** variant pages show a dispatch window about 4–6 working days out ("Versanddatum"); DoorBird quotes 7–15 working days.

### Where Metzler is behind
1. **Preview coverage.** About 85 % of products, including about 98 % of Türklingeln, have no live preview. Frabox and gravuru have one on every doorbell.
2. **Delivery promise.**
   - No delivery estimate on cards.
   - The PDP shows "Versanddatum" (a ship date) only after picking a variant.
   - No cut-off time.
   - Letterman, Frabox and OBI do better.
3. **Guided selling only on Sprechanlagen.** None for Briefkasten, Paketbox or Klingel (number of parties, Unterputz vs Aufputz, Zeitungsfach).
4. **No compatibility / retrofit check, AR, or save/share** of a configuration. DoorBird, Nuki, Ring and Siedle have these.
5. **XDM10 PDP wastes its Red Dot and has no reviews.** There is no Red Dot landing page (/reddot returns 404) and /auszeichnungen is outdated.
6. **Consistency of claims.** Customer counts differ (2 Mio. / 1,8 Mio. / 1 Mio.). The "Kauf auf Rechnung" USP is only via Klarna (direct Rechnung is for Behörden only). The guarantee is shown on electronics but covers Durchrostung only. Rivals with fewer claims look cleaner.
